# Corrected extra experiments

These experiments use the corrected 909-view manifest, with 135 test views in 117 source groups. View-level results include full frames and extracted symptom views. Source grouping reduces known overlap. Unknown plant/session identities remain a limitation.

## Reproduce

Run .\rerun_extras.ps1 from the D-drive project after setting up source datasets, corrected checkpoints and the fresh environment. The launcher sets temporary and model/plot caches to D. It runs all six modules and stops on errors. The first feature extraction is cached under .runtime; changes to the manifest, relevant preprocessing implementations, model or package versions invalidate it. Rebuilding base checkpoints uses rebuild_dip.ps1. These studies preserve the default demo checkpoints.

## Methods and outputs

- rf_feature_ablation.csv compares all seven nonempty HOG/LBP/colour subsets with 300-tree Random Forests and fixed seed 42. Training weights balance class and source group. Validation macro-F1 selects LBP+Colour, with test accuracy 91.11% and macro-F1 0.8948. Colour-only test accuracy is higher, but it was not the validation-selected subset.
- augmentation_comparison.csv and svm_augmentation.csv compare original training views with one deterministic geometric/photometric augmentation per view. Original and augmented copies share the parent's total sample weight. Validation/test views remain unchanged. SVM C is selected separately from 0.1, 1 and 10 using validation. RF test macro-F1 decreases from 0.8864 to 0.8781. SVM test macro-F1 increases from 0.7722 to 0.7864, while validation macro-F1 decreases. This mixed result does not justify replacing the default model.
- embedding_svm.csv measures an SVM on the frozen ImageNet MobileNet embeddings, selecting C on validation. Test accuracy is 93.33% and macro-F1 0.9329. The corrected DIP MobileNet head retains higher accuracy at 95.56%.
- leaf_miner_learning_curve.csv uses nested subsets of 15, 30, 44 and 59 Leaf Miner training source groups with seeds 42, 43 and 44. Other classes stay fixed, and train weights are recomputed per subset. SVM C stays at the corrected baseline's 10. Mean test Leaf Miner recall rises from 22.62% to 71.43%. Group composition and view count change together. Plant/session independence is unverified. learning_curve.png shows all seeded curves.
- feature_tsne.png and tsne_coordinates.csv show separate HOG and MobileNet projections of the same 135 test views. PCA reduces each matrix to 30 components, then t-SNE uses perplexity 20 and seed 42. The maps illustrate neighborhoods. Axis locations and inter-cluster distances are projection-dependent. Metric comparisons remain the basis for model selection.
- quantized_storage.csv compares the corrected DIP checkpoint with per-output-channel symmetric INT8 weight storage. File size falls from 9,166,603 to 2,654,416 bytes, a 71.04% reduction. Accuracy falls from 95.56% to 94.81%. Weights are restored to FP32 for evaluation. This experiment measures checkpoint compression, with no INT8 speed claim.
- stress_predictions.csv and stress_summary.csv use matched source images for raw and DIP inference. Brightness is multiplied by 0.6. Gaussian noise has standard deviation 25 on the 0–255 RGB scale with clipping, applied before DIP using three fixed seeds. Source resolution influences the noise remaining after resizing. Mean noisy accuracy is 96.54% raw and 90.62% DIP. Both achieve 97.78% on the darker inputs. These are synthetic stress conditions, with no claim of independent field validation. stress_comparison.png shows mean accuracy and standard deviation across noise seeds, which are not confidence intervals.
- legacy_tensor_noise_predictions.csv and legacy_tensor_noise_summary.csv rerun the historical post-normalization noise condition with standard deviation 0.8 and three paired seeds. Noise affects leaf pixels and masked background. Mean accuracy is 75.31% raw and 67.65% DIP. The original experiment's DIP advantage does not reproduce on the corrected models.
- gradcam_*.png contains eight examples selected before heatmap inspection. Selection takes the first correct DIP prediction per class, preferring full frames, and the first error where available. Every panel shows raw/DIP inputs, predictions, predicted-class CAM and true-class CAM. Correct predictions have identical predicted/true targets. Last-layer Grad-CAM is spatially coarse. Visual review found plausible leaf focus in some examples, along with background responses and missed damage sites.
- gradcam_checks.csv records the target-score drop after replacing the hottest 20% of pixels with ImageNet mean colour, versus three random equal-area deletions. On distinct selected view/target pairs, hot-region deletion exceeds random deletion in 8/11 DIP cases and 2/9 raw cases. These small, selected samples and out-of-distribution deletions provide a diagnostic faithfulness check. They do not establish symptom localization.
- leaf_miner_localization.csv compares true-class CAM with the publisher's damage rectangles on all 28 held-out WMC Leaf Miner views. Mean CAM-energy enrichment relative to uniform energy is 1.87 for DIP crops versus 1.33 raw. Full-frame enrichment is about 1.01 DIP and 0.93 raw. Neither model's peak lands in a damage box on any of the four full frames. Rectangles include surrounding tissue. Crop/full-frame views can share source groups. Precise lesion masks and equivalent annotations for the other classes are unavailable in this check.
- extra_comparisons.png combines ablation, augmentation and checkpoint-size charts.
- verification_summary.json records baseline reproduction and aggregate checks. Cached raw MobileNet, DIP MobileNet and DIP SVM predictions were checked against all 135 saved test predictions. Clean stress predictions also match both saved MobileNet baselines.

## Interpretation

The extra work establishes specific trade-offs and failure modes. Storage compression and the source-group learning curve show measurable benefits. Augmentation has mixed effects, hybrid SVM does not outperform the neural head, and the corrected stress tests favor raw inference. Grad-CAM demonstrates some foreground focus but does not establish reliable whole-leaf damage localization. The class/host/source limitations remain.

Hyperparameters and feature-subset selection use validation. Test curves and diagnostics are exploratory comparisons on the same held-out split and should be identified accordingly. No model was selected from attractive heatmaps or modified to make these visualizations appear successful.
